Since 2019

Considered decor for quieter homes

Homeable began as a small studio sourcing handmade ceramics from independent makers. Today we work with a curated group of artisans and small manufacturers who share our belief that a home should feel calm, not cluttered.

Every piece in our collection is chosen for its material honesty — natural stoneware, solid oak, hand-blown glass, undyed linen — and for how it ages, not just how it photographs.
